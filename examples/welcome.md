# Welcome to .MD Viewer

This sample folder shows what the viewer can do. All content is fictional.

## What to try

- Open other files from the tree on the left.
- Press **Ctrl+K** to search names and contents, for example the word `diagram`.
- Look at the outline on the right: it highlights the section you are in.
- Click **Edit** to see the source next to a live preview. **Ctrl+S** saves.

## Links between documents

Relative links open the other file inside the viewer:

- [Formatting guide](guide/formatting.md)
- [Diagrams](guide/diagrams.md)
- [Project plan](project/plan.md)

> Documents are read from and saved to your own folder: there is no database and no cloud in between.
