# Sample project plan

## Goal

Launch a first version for a small group of users and measure usage before expanding.

## Phases

| Phase | Duration | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Discovery | 2 weeks | Validated problems |
| 2. Prototype | 4 weeks | Clickable version |
| 3. Pilot | 6 weeks | First real users |

## Risks

- The team may not be able to cover support during the pilot.
- Sample data may not represent real cases.
- The flow diagram may change: see [diagrams](../guide/diagrams.md).

## Next steps

1. Decide who takes part in the pilot.
2. Prepare **synthetic** test data.
3. Set a review date.
